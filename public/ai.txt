# About
Since AI is a global, execution-focused AI innovation ecosystem, run by the
Finnish non-profit Since AI ry and based in Turku, Finland. We support AI projects from challenge ideation through hackathon
building to production deployment and commercialization.

# What Makes Us Different
Unlike traditional hackathons (weekend event → demo → nothing), Since AI provides:
1. Pre-event: Challenge ideation with companies
2. Event: 48-hour intensive hackathon
3. Post-event: 8+ weeks production deployment support
4. Commercialization: Startup support, investor connections
5. Open Source: open code and community challenges for European AI
6. Academic Bridge: Research commercialization pathway

# The Since AI Innovation Lifecycle
Phase 1: Challenge Ideation — Co-create challenges with partner companies
Phase 2: Intensive Building — 48-hour hackathon with production focus
Phase 3: Production Support — 8+ weeks of deployment assistance
Phase 4: Commercialization — Startup formation, investor connections via Antler
